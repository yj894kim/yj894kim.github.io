01 / About

How to
play.

Guide your worm through the field, collect glowing food, and grow by eating smaller worms. A larger worm collision ends the round.

Use the arrow keys or WASD on desktop, or the on-screen D-pad on touch devices. Choose a pattern, press Start game, and use Pause whenever you need a break.
